# Changelog

## 0.5.1 (17 November 2026)

- The Mac download no longer opens as "damaged". You still need Open Anyway the first time.

### Known issues

- The apps aren't signed yet, so you'll see a warning the first time you open one.
- On a Mac you may be asked for your password after each update.
- Updates aren't automatic yet. Get new versions from this page.
- On Linux there's no app lock, and HEIC photos can't be added.

## 0.5.0 (16 November 2026)

First beta.

- Write notes with text, checklists and photos. They sync between your own devices and are never stored on our server.
- Runs on Mac, Windows, Linux and Android phones and tablets.
- Add a device by scanning a QR code or typing a short code.
- Keep your recovery kit somewhere safe. Its 12 words bring your notes back if you lose every device.
- Search finds words anywhere in a note.
- Folders can go inside folders.
- Deleted notes stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days.
- You can lock the app and encrypt your backups in Settings.
- Devices on the same Wi-Fi can sync even when the internet is down.

### Known issues

- The apps aren't signed yet, so you'll see a warning the first time you open one.
- On a Mac you may be asked for your password after each update.
- Updates aren't automatic yet. Get new versions from this page.
- On Linux there's no app lock, and HEIC photos can't be added.
